FAQ

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Starting your fitness journey should feel simple. Find answers about memberships, facilities, personal training, and what to expect at Ironvale Gym.

Is Ironvale Gym suitable for beginners?

Yes. Every new member gets an intro session with a coach who builds a plan around your level, goals and schedule.

Can I try the gym before becoming a member?

Yes, we offer trial options so you can experience our facilities, atmosphere and training environment before choosing a membership. Book a free trial below.

What is included in a membership?

All plans include gym floor access, lockers and showers. Higher plans add unlimited classes, progress check-ins, personal training and nutrition support.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes. Choose a coach from our team and book one-off sessions or a monthly 1:1 package.

How do I choose the right training program?

Book a free consultation. A coach will assess your goals and recommend the program and plan that fits you best.
